Lakeman’s Lookout

The following snippet is taken from the Mountain Forecast section of FAR’s website. Bear in mind that it will be up to 24 hours old by the time you read it! Many thanks as always to Ron Lakeman for his hard work.

Mountain Weather Report


ISSUED: Thursday January 29, 2025 11:00 MST

SYNOPSIS: Another system will push inland from the coast and largely dissipate, as it drifts over an upper ridge of high pressure, for variable cloud to mainly cloudy skies and a 60% chance of flurries at times across the Fernie area today and the majority of tonight. A larger Pacific system should follow for occasional snow (fluctuating snow level) and increased wind at times late overnight, Friday and Friday evening. The snow should ease off later Friday night.

FREEZING LEVEL: Rising to between 1100 & 1400 metres this afternoon. At or near surface tonight. Rising to between 1400 & 1700 metres Friday.

FERNIE ALPINE RESORT FORECAST (1615 metres):

TODAY: Cloudy with the odd sunny break and morning fog patches. 60% chance of flurries during the late morning and afternoon. Minor accumulation possible. High near -1.

TONIGHT: Mainly cloudy. 60% chance of flurries during the evening, a few flurries or occasional snow developing near morning. Minor accumulation possible. Slight chance of freezing rain. Low near -2.

FRIDAY: Cloudy with occasional snow. Snow level rising to between 1400 & 1600 metres. Light accumulation, possible 4 to 8 cm. Slight chance of freezing rain during the morning. Windy at times. High near 1.

FRIDAY NIGHT: Occasional snow and possible snow showers during the evening, easing off overnight. Snow level trending lower. Light accumulation, possible 3 to 8 cm. Windy at times. Low -1 or -2.

OUTLOOK:

SATURDAY: Cloudy with sunny periods, possible morning fog patches. High 1 or 2. Increasing cloud and a slight chance of flurries Saturday night. Low near -2.

SUNDAY: Cloudy with possible morning fog patches and sunny periods. High 1 or 2. 60% chance of flurries or occasional snow Sunday night, light accumulation possible. Low near -2.

MONDAY: Occasional snow or a few flurries and possible snow showers during the morning. Light accumulation possible. Cloudy with possible sunny breaks during the afternoon. High 1 or 2. Low near -2.

CONFIDENCE/DISCUSSION: Fair confidence. All of the guidance is now downplaying the chance of more than a very light dusting of snow with todays dissipating disturbance. The moisture content of the following system still appears greater and the computer continue to advance its timing. Occasional snow could develop after 4 or 5 AM Friday. The milder trending southwesterly flow is a small risk for light freezing rain tonight and early Friday and then makes for some uncertainty of the snow level by Friday afternoon. An upper trough and its unstable airmass should follow the cold front for possible snow showers and a dropping snow level Friday evening. High pressure should briefly rebuild over southern BC Saturday. The leading edge of Pacific system could clip the area Saturday night. The trailing cold front and a following upper trough are currently timed for late Sunday night and Monday morning, this could easily change. High pressure could rebuild Monday afternoon-Monday night.

 

Ron Lakeman.
