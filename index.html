Avalanche Bulletin

Updated on 2026-02-23 17:00:00 MST, valid until 2026-02-24 17:00:00 MST.

Valid for: Crawford-Flathead-Lizard-Moyie-St. Mary.

Summary
Stick to conservative, low consequence terrain and give the storm snow time to settle. Recent storm snow has weakened a concerning upper snowpack, human triggered avalanches are likely.
  • Continue to make conservative terrain choices while the storm snow settles and stabilizes.
  • Keep your guard up at lower elevations. Storms slabs have been reactive at all elevations.
  • Storm slabs in motion may step down to deeper layers resulting in large avalanches.
  • Be aware of the potential for remote triggering and large avalanches due to buried surface hoar.
  • Be aware of the potential for loose avalanches in steep terrain where snow hasn't formed a slab.

Further Detail
Avalanche Summary

Numerous storm and persistent slab avalanches up to size 3 were reported in the region over the past 4 days. Avalanches have been triggered accidentally, naturally, and remotely. These avalanches have occurred on all aspects and elevations but most activity has been on north and east facing terrain. Check out this MIN report and this one for more details.

Snowpack Summary

Up to 20 cm of fresh snow accompanied by strong southwest wind has formed deeper deposits on north and east aspects. In sheltered terrain it will overlie a layer of surface hoar or a sun crust. The snow surface is expected to become moist on sun exposed slopes. A couple concerning weak layers exist in the upper snowpack:

  • 60 to 80 cm deep, there is a supportive crust on south-facing terrain. On north-facing terrain, this layer maybe a breakable crust or surface hoar.

  • The late January layer is buried 70 to 120 cm deep. It consists of a crust with facets or surface hoar above it.

The mid and lower snowpack is well settled.

ISSUED: Monday February 23, 2025 11:00 MST

SYNOPSIS: An upper trough will push eastward across the area with flurries, possible snow showers and gusty winds today. A few flurries or snow showers should linger early this evening. Fernie should generally reside between disturbances for partial clearing overnight and variable cloud or partly cloudy skies Tuesday. A disturbance could drift just north of the International Border for increased cloud cover and a 40% chance of flurries Tuesday evening. The temperature will be milder.

FREEZING LEVEL: Fluctuating between 1100 & 1600 metres today. Dropping to surface tonight. Rising to between 1400 & 1600 metres Tuesday afternoon.

TUESDAY: Cloudy with sunny breaks or sunny periods, possible morning fog patches. High near 0.

TUESDAY NIGHT: Variable cloud and a 40% chance of flurries during the evening. Cloudy with clear periods overnight. Low near -6.

OUTLOOK:

WEDNESDAY: Cloudy with sunny breaks. 60-70% chance of afternoon and evening flurries. Minor to low end light accumulation possible. Increasing wind during the afternoon. High near -2. Low -2 or -3.

THURSDAY and THURSDAY NIGHT: Cloudy with a few flurries and then occasional snow. Light accumulation possible. Windy, strong at times. High near 0. Low near -4.

FRIDAY: Cloudy with a 60% chance of flurries or occasional snow. Minor to light accumulation possible. Windy at times. High -2 or -3. Low near -10.

CONFIDENCE/DISCUSSION: Fair confidence except poor to fair confidence of the local amount of snow today, tonight and again Wednesday afternoon onward. The computer models are increasingly variable with the possibility of convective snow showers and the resulting amount of snow today and this evening. Some solutions are now trending lighter. The most recent guidance is in better agreement that precipitation associated with the Tuesday afternoon-Tuesday night disturbance will be largely confined south of Fernie. The timing and moisture content of a series of disturbances entrained in a strong west-northwesterly flow aloft Wednesday afternoon through Friday evening are questionable. Most of the guidance suggests strong wind at times and also favours Thursday and Thursday night for accumulating snow, however there is some uncertainty of the snow level Thursday.
